# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the school activities site.

## Before you start

- Make sure the change supports a school activity or helps maintain the site.
- Do not add private student information, passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive data.
- Check that names, dates, room numbers, and contact details are accurate before requesting review.

## Making a change

1. Create a branch with a descriptive name, such as `add-art-club-event`.
2. Make the smallest change that solves the problem.
3. Check the page, links, spelling, and formatting locally.
4. Open a pull request that explains what changed and why.
5. Request review from a second teacher.
6. Address review comments and wait for the required approvals before merging.

## Review expectations

Reviewers should check that:

- The information is appropriate for a school audience.
- No confidential or unnecessary personal information is included.
- Links work and dates are clear.
- The page is readable on a phone and uses meaningful headings and labels.
- The change does not unintentionally remove or alter another activity.

The pull-request template provides a checklist for the second-teacher review.
